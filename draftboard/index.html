Export

To save the image, right-click or long-press the preview. Downloads are blocked when Draftboard runs inside a viewer, so copying is the reliable route.

Shortcuts

Drag an arrow from one shape to another and it stays attached when you move either shape. Double-click any shape or arrow to label it. Paste images, text, or copied Excalidraw shapes straight onto the board.
